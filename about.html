More Than Furniture.

FAQ

Questions people ask us.

What does A to Z Furniture Polish do? +

A to Z Furniture Polish is a furniture store and furniture polishing workshop in Jogeshwari West, Mumbai. We sell modern furniture, make custom furniture, and provide professional furniture polishing, refinishing and restoration.

Where are you located and which areas do you serve? +

We are at Gala No. 312, Sahara Building, Near SRK Hospital, Oshiwara, Jogeshwari West, Mumbai 400102. We serve Mumbai, including Oshiwara, Jogeshwari West and Andheri West.

How much does furniture polishing cost? +

Polishing starts from ₹999 for a 2 seater sofa frame, ₹1,999 for chairs, ₹2,999 for a sofa bed and ₹3,999 for dining tables, beds and wardrobes. The final price depends on the size and condition of the furniture, and we give a free quote.

How do I get a quote for polishing? +

Send us a photo of your furniture on WhatsApp at +91 81085 82270 or call us. We look at the condition and recommend the right polishing or refinishing service.

Can you make custom furniture? +

Yes. Share your idea, measurements or a reference image and we design the furniture around your space. The process is simple: discuss, create, deliver.

Which furniture can you polish or restore? +

We polish and restore wooden furniture such as sofa frames, chairs, dining tables, beds, sofa beds, TV units, wardrobes and kitchen shelves.

How long does delivery take? +

Each product page shows an estimated delivery time. For the listed furniture it ranges from about 7 to 25 days depending on the piece. For custom work, timing is confirmed after we discuss your requirements.
